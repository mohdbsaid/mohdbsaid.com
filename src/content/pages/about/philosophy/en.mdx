Good additive manufacturing work is mostly invisible — a part that prints right the first time, a machine that doesn't go down mid-run, a workshop attendee who leaves able to fix their own printer instead of needing to call someone. I'd rather spend an extra ten minutes finding why a print actually failed than spend an hour reprinting it and hoping. That habit is slower in the moment and faster over a career.
