Independent of any single employer, I've spent years designing and delivering training on additive manufacturing fundamentals — printer operation, maintenance, safety, slicing, and practical applications — for teachers, students, and industry professionals across Oman. Some of that work has been with schools and universities adopting 3D printing as part of hands-on STEM learning; some with professionals looking to bring the skill in-house. Every session is shaped around where the audience actually is, not a fixed curriculum.
